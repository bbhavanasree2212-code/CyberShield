Online safety checklist

✓ Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

✓ Never share an OTP, PIN or banking password.

✓ Verify payment requests and links before opening them.

✓ Keep screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers and relevant messages as evidence.

✓ Contact your bank immediately if you suspect financial fraud.

Frequently asked questions

How do I report an online financial scam?

Contact India's cybercrime helpline at 1930 as soon as possible and submit details through the official portal at cybercrime.gov.in. Contact your bank or payment provider as well.

What evidence should I keep?

Keep screenshots, payment receipts, transaction references, suspicious URLs, phone numbers, emails and relevant chat messages. Do not alter the original evidence.

What should I do if my account is compromised?

Change your password from a trusted device, secure your recovery email, enable two-factor authentication, sign out other sessions and contact the affected service provider.

Does this page send a support ticket?

No. This page provides contact information and a draft-message helper. It does not store or submit support tickets to a server.
